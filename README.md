# Hi, I'm Kirk

I build mobile apps with .NET MAUI and Android, mostly small, privacy-first tools that keep your data on your own phone. I also contribute fixes to open-source projects I use.

## Apps I've built

- **[Push-up Track](https://github.com/KirkPatrickJunsay/Push-Up-Track)**: counts your push-ups and grades your form with on-device pose AI.
- **[Fast Track](https://github.com/KirkPatrickJunsay/fast-track)**: an intermittent fasting tracker that stays entirely on your phone.
- **[Walk Track](https://github.com/KirkPatrickJunsay/Walk-Base)**: a step tracker that turns your walking into a small town you build.
- **[Suikoden Codex](https://github.com/KirkPatrickJunsay/Suikoden-Codex)**: an offline fan encyclopedia and companion app for Suikoden I–V.
- **[Blip Me](https://github.com/KirkPatrickJunsay/Blip-Me)**: phone notifications for long-running coding agents, over your own network.
